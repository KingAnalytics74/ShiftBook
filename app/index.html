      Privacy

      Last updated 30 October 2026

      In short

      Shiftbook keeps your shift, pay and money details so it can work out your pay. We don't sell your data, show ads, track you with cookies or share anything with your employer.

      Who we are

      Shiftbook is made by WesTech Innovation, which is responsible for this app. Contact us through westechinnovation.com.

      What Shiftbook keeps

      • What you enter in setup: your first name, role, hourly and sleep-in rates, pay period start day and usual shift times.
      • Your profile: age group, sex (optional), your area, your employer (optional) and your community nickname. Other carers only ever see your nickname. Your employer never sees any of it. Age group and sex are only used, added up across all users, to understand who uses Shiftbook.
      • How you sign in: your mobile number or email address and, if you choose one, a password. If you continue with Google, Google tells us your email address and name, and nothing else. Your password is only ever kept scrambled (hashed) by Supabase, so nobody at Shiftbook can read it.
      • Your shifts: dates, hours, breaks, sleep-ins, notes, and the service users you choose to add.
      • Payslip figures you type in, your tax settings, your spending plan and spending amounts.
      • In the community: your public profile (nickname and area only), your posts, and messages you send. Messages to the Shiftbook team are read only by you and the team.

      Shiftbook does not ask for your bank details, National Insurance number, address or date of birth, and it isn't connected to your bank.

      Service users

      Please use first names or initials only for the people you support. Don't add health details or other personal information about them in notes.

      Where your data is kept and who can see it

      When you sign in, your data is kept in Shiftbook's database, run on Supabase. It's tied to your account and locked so that only you can read or change it. Supabase stores and processes it for us under its own privacy policy.

      In demo mode, nothing is sent anywhere: your data stays in this browser on this device.

      Nobody else using Shiftbook can see your data: not other carers and not your employer. The only exceptions are what you choose to share: your posts and profile in the community, private messages (only you and the carer you're talking to can read them, unless one of you reports a message to the moderators), and messages to the Shiftbook team.

      Cookies and storage on your device

      Shiftbook doesn't use cookies for advertising or tracking, and has no analytics. It keeps a few things in your browser: which tab you last had open, so the app opens where you left off. When you're signed in it also keeps your sign-in session, so you stay signed in; in demo mode it keeps all your Shiftbook data in this browser. Fonts and code come from Shiftbook's own site, so nothing is requested from Google or other third parties. The only other service Shiftbook talks to is Supabase, which stores your data.

      Because nothing is used for tracking, there's no cookie banner. If that ever changes, we'll ask you first.

      Why we use it

      Only to give you the app: working out your pay, take-home, rest check, charts and money plan. Your information isn't used for anything else.

      Your rights

      • See and download everything: Timesheet → Back up all data.
      • Correct anything: edit a shift, payslip or setting at any time.
      • Delete a single shift from its edit screen, or everything with the button below.

      You also have rights under UK data protection law. If you're unhappy with how your data is handled, contact us, or complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

      How long it's kept

      Until you delete it. If this version of Shiftbook closes, you'll be told in advance so you can download your data.

      Estimates, not advice

      Pay, tax and rest figures are estimates to help you plan. They are not financial, tax or legal advice. Your payslip is the final word.

      Delete my account

      This deletes your account and all your shifts, payslips, spending and settings. It can't be undone, so download a backup first if you want one.

      Questions and answers

      What is Shiftbook?
      A shift log for care and support workers. Log each shift, including sleep-ins and breaks, and Shiftbook shows your gross pay, an estimate of your take-home, a check against your payslip, a rest warning and a plan for your money.
      How is my pay worked out?
      For each shift: (day hours − unpaid break) × your hourly rate, plus your sleep-in rate if it was a sleep-in. Your rates come from Settings on the Timesheet tab.
      Is my take-home exact?
      It's an estimate using 2026/27 tax, National Insurance and pension rules for England, Wales and Northern Ireland. Your payslip is the final word. Add your payslip on the Money tab and Shiftbook compares it line by line.
      My payslip doesn't match. What should I do?
      Check the hours and sleep-ins logged for that pay period first. If the payslip shows no sleep-in pay but you did sleep-ins, or fewer hours than you worked, ask your payroll team. The payslip check shows exactly which line is different.
      Why is my Rest check red?
      Red means you're not getting enough rest: 7 or more days in a row without a day off, more than 48 hours in 7 days, or a day shift, sleep-in and another day shift back to back. It's a guide based on UK working time rules, not legal advice.
      What does 'unpaid breaks worth' mean?
      The hours of unpaid breaks you took, multiplied by your hourly rate. It shows the time you give at work without pay.
      How do I change or delete a shift?
      Tap the day on the calendar or its row on the Timesheet tab. Change the details and tap Save changes, or tap Delete. If you only worked part of a shift, change the hours instead of deleting it.
      How do I send my timesheet to my manager?
      Timesheet tab → Download CSV. The file includes your name, the pay period, every shift and the totals. It opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
      Can my employer see my data?
      No. Your data is private to your account. Your employer, your manager and other carers can't see it. Only what you choose to send, such as a downloaded timesheet, leaves the app.
      I forgot my password. What do I do?
      On the sign-in screen, tap Sign in with email and password, then Forgot your password? We'll email you a link to choose a new one. It works for one hour. You can also sign in with a code sent to your email instead. If you signed up with an email code, the same link lets you add a password.
      Can I use Shiftbook on more than one device?
      Yes. Sign in with the same phone number, email or password on your phone or computer and you'll see the same data. In demo mode, data stays on one device.
      Should I put service users' full names in?
      No. Use first names or initials only. Shiftbook only needs to tell your service users apart, and it's kinder to their privacy.
      Does it work for weekly pay, Scotland or two jobs?
      Not yet. Shiftbook currently supports monthly pay periods, one hourly and one sleep-in rate, and tax rules for England, Wales and Northern Ireland. These are on the roadmap.
