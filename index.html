Questions

Is Shiftbuck really free?

Yes. It's free for every worker, with no ads. We don't sell your data.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You type in your shifts and your plans yourself, so there is no bank link to set up and nothing to trust us with.

Can my employer see my Shiftbuck?

No. Your data is private to your account. Your employer, your manager and other workers can't see it. Only what you choose to send, such as a downloaded timesheet, leaves the app.

What do other members see about me?

Only your nickname and your council area, and only if you join the community. Never your real name, your workplace or your pay.

Is it only for care workers?

No. Shiftbuck works for care, factory and warehouse work, and for anyone paid by the shift.
